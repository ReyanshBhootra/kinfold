HALE, Frances M. (née Mercer)
Frances Mercer Hale, 88, of Trenton, New Jersey, died peacefully at home on March 3, 2008.
Born in 1920 in Trenton, Frances was the daughter of the late
Walter and Mary Mercer. She worked for many years at the Roebling
mill and sang in the choir at St. Mary's.

Frances was predeceased by her husband of 51 years, Edward Hale, and by
her brother, Arthur Mercer.

Frances is survived by her son, George Hale, and his wife Patricia, of Camden;
and by her beloved granddaughter, Lena Hale. Frances will be remembered
for her garden and her stubborn kindness.

Visitation Saturday 10-11 AM, Mercer-Hale Funeral Home, Trenton.
