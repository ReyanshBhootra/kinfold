COURIER-POST (Camden, N.J.) · June 9, 1972 · "Births" column
[SYNTHETIC RECORD FOR THE KINFOLD DEMO. ALL PEOPLE ARE FICTIONAL.]

Mr. and Mrs. George Hale, of 214 Linden St., Camden, New Jersey, announce
the birth of their first child, a daughter, Lena Hale, born June 4, 1972,
at Cooper Hospital. Mrs. Hale is the former Patricia Novak of Collingswood.
Paternal grandparents are Mr. and Mrs. Edward Hale of Trenton.
