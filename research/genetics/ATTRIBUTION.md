# Attribution and prior work

- **Dataset:** Williams, Amy L. (2025-10-31). *Ped-sim def files and simulated segments from100,000 pairs of1st through8th cousins*. Zenodo, DOI[10.5281/zenodo.17489849](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17489849). Public metadata lists [CC BY4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit belongs to the creator. Local transformation selects full-cousin summary members, restores documented omitted zero slots, excludes one unresolved source anomaly, bins measurements and creates separate train/calibration/test groups. No suggestion of creator endorsement.
- **Simulator:** [Williams Lab Ped-sim](https://github.com/williamslab/ped-sim), existing simulator, GPL3.0 code license. We did not run or modify its executable/source; source IBD summaries/definitions are attributed separately under the dataset's license. The supplied README.sh was inspected as data, not executed.
- **Existing pedigree inference:** [Bonsai](https://github.com/23andMe/bonsaitree) already builds pedigrees using IBD, age and sex information. It is related work, not our invention or an implemented baseline here.
- **Existing error-tolerant reconstruction:** Huang, Li and Narasimhan (2026), [*Fault-tolerant pedigree reconstruction from pairwise kinship relations*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13293059/), Bioinformatics42(6),btag251, DOI10.1093/bioinformatics/btag251. Its reconstruction/error-tolerance and contextual constraints mean those broad ideas are not new here. No claim that our eight-class classifier outperforms this full-pedigree method.
- **Existing interactive genealogy guidance:** [*FIGGsolver: An interactive algorithm that guides the genealogy process in forensic investigative genetic genealogy*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42999560/),2026. The primary indexed title was verified; full-method comparison was not completed in this bounded run. We do not claim to invent next-action genealogy guidance.
- **Existing uncertainty methods:** Angelopoulos and Bates, [*A Gentle Introduction to Conformal Prediction and Distribution-Free Uncertainty Quantification*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07511), tutorial/preprint; Gibbs, Cherian and Candès, [*Conformal Prediction With Conditional Guarantees*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.12616). Split/conformal prediction and conditional-coverage techniques are existing methods, not new contributions of this prototype.

This small source review is not an exhaustive novelty search. Source-provenance-aware robustness for particular record/verification workflows remains a proposed research question. Nothing here demonstrates publication novelty, a new biological mechanism, forensic validity or sponsor/prize eligibility.
